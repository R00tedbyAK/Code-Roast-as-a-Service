### Project Description
Backspace Per Minute (BPM) is a satirical, single-page web app that parodies traditional typing tools by tracking your hesitation and self-doubt instead of speed. Built with React and Tailwind CSS, it intentionally hides WPM and accuracy metrics, measuring only how many times you press backspace to deliver a hilarious "personality diagnosis" based on your keypress regret.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Typing Speed & Efficiency: It completely refuses to track Words Per Minute (WPM), net speed, or typing efficiency—doing nothing to help you type faster.
Grammar & Accuracy: It ignores spelling errors, missing punctuation, and accuracy scores—offering zero constructive feedback on your writing quality.
Ergonomic & Physical Strain: It does not track hand position, wrist posture, or physical strain—leaving your keyboard health entirely unmonitored.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
Microsecond Hesitation Tracking: Every time your finger hovers over the backspace key in sheer panic, our high-precision event listeners record your self-doubt in real time.

The "Zero-Value" Dashboard: We actively strip away every useful metric (WPM, net speed, accuracy percentage) and replace them with raw, unfiltered Backspaces Per Minute (BPM).
